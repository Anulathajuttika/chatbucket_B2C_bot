## 1. Overview

**ChatBucket** is a **next-generation chatting application** designed to enhance
user communication through a **seamless, secure, and feature-rich platform**.
Integrated with **advanced AI models**, the app offers intelligent features like
**message translation, voice-to-text conversion, and personalized user
experiences**, setting it apart from traditional messaging apps.

Tagline / brand line: **"ChatBucket — Global connectivity."**

**Major feature areas (map of this document):**
1. Messaging & Communication
2. Audio Calling
3. Video Call System
4. Status (Stories)
5. Live Streaming
6. Nearby
7. Weather
8. **ToDoZee AI** — the built-in AI assistant (29 features across 8 categories)

ChatBucket also has a **built-in AI assistant called ToDoZee** (see Section 8).
For a deep-dive on ToDoZee, a separate knowledge file exists; this document
summarizes ToDoZee as it appears inside ChatBucket.

## 2. Messaging & Communication

- **P2P, Group & Channel Chats** — Direct messaging, group chats, and
  public/private channels.
- **Channel & Group Creation** — Users can create channels and groups with
  customizable names, bios, and profile pictures.
- **Group & Channel Management** — Admins control bio, wallpaper, permissions,
  and pinned messages.
- **Events** — Create event-based groups where members can chat and coordinate.
  Each event has a **set expiry date**, after which the event chat automatically
  closes.
- **End-to-End Encryption** — All chats (P2P, group, and channel messages) are
  **encrypted by default** for enhanced security.
- **View Once Message** — Send photos, videos, or messages that **disappear after
  being viewed**. The sender can also set a **custom view duration** for added
  privacy.
- **Smart Send** — Send a message to **multiple people at once**. Users can save
  selected recipients as **Smart Groups** with a custom name for quick future
  messaging.
- **Translation** — Incoming messages are **automatically translated** into the
  selected language when enabled. (Includes a "Show original" option.)
- **Message Editing & Deletion** — Edit or delete messages **within 15 minutes**
  of sending.
- **Scheduled Messages & Calls** — Schedule messages, stories, and calls **up to
  30 days in advance**.
- **Message Forwarding** — Forward messages to **up to 50 recipients**, including
  individuals, groups, and channels.
- **Secret Chats** — End-to-end encrypted, **self-destructing** chats.
- **Chat Locking & Archiving** — Lock chats for privacy and archive for later.
- **Blocking & Reporting** — Block users and report chats (with required input).
- **Clear Chat** — Quickly clear messages from any conversation.
- **Mute Conversations** — Silence notifications for specific chats.
- **Archive Chats** — Store inactive chats without deleting them.
- **Delete Conversations** — Permanently remove chats from history.
- **Pin Conversations** — Keep important chats at the top for easy access.
- **Chat Analysis** — View insights on **message activity, engagement, and
  trends** (available only for **groups, channels, and business profiles**).
- **User Badges & Tiers** — Special badges for **Tier 2 & Tier 3** users.
- **Emojis, Stickers & Premium Features** — Regular emojis plus **exclusive
  stickers**.
- **Unique Username & QR Code** — Shareable **QR code** for easy contact sharing.

**Privacy & control settings noted in the app:** granular Last-seen, Profile
photo, Bio, Stories, Phone number, Calls, Messages, Forward-message, and
Group/Channel-invitation visibility (Everyone / My contacts / My contacts except
/ Nobody); Read receipts; App lock; Disappearing messages (24 hours / 7 days /
90 days / Off).

## 3. Audio Calling

- **One-to-One Audio Calling**
  - High-quality private audio calls with another user.
  - **Adaptive bitrate streaming** for smooth audio across network conditions.
  - Mute/unmute audio as needed.
  - **AI-powered voice cloning and language translation** for a more natural,
    personalized conversation.

- **Group Audio Calling**
  - Supports audio calls with **up to 10 participants**.
  - Dynamic audio-quality adjustment for optimal performance.
  - Participants can **join and leave without disrupting** the call.
  - AI-powered voice cloning for personalized interactions.
  - **Per-participant** AI-powered **real-time translation** (each participant can
    have their own translation language).
  - Mute others' audio for yourself without affecting others.
  - **Screen sharing** allowed for up to 2 users simultaneously.

- **HD Audio**
  - High-definition audio quality with **low latency**.
  - **Noise cancellation** for clear communication.

- **Speech-to-Speech & AI-Powered Voice Translation with Voice Cloning**
  - AI-driven **real-time language translation** between supported languages.
  - Users select their preferred language for AI translation.
  - Advanced AI voice synthesis for a **natural, lifelike** conversation.
  - **Voice cloning maintains the speaker's unique voice** while translating.

- **Audio Soundboard**
  - Play **pre-recorded sound effects** during calls.
  - Customizable soundboard with volume control.

- **Convert Audio Call to Video Call**
  - **One-click transition** from audio to video.
  - No call disconnection required during conversion.
  - Video call follows the same group limit (**max 10 users**).

- **Group & Channel Audio Podcasts**
  - Host **live audio podcasts** within groups and channels.
  - Listeners can **join, react, send messages, and participate** in discussions.
  - Podcasts can be **recorded and stored for on-demand playback**.
  - All group members can join and engage in the live session.
  - **Live Chat Support:** participants send real-time messages to share thoughts
    and ask questions.
  - **Admin Controls:** add, remove, or block users from the current session;
    manage audience participation, control settings, and moderate live chat.

- **Call Scheduling & Reminders**
  - Schedule **one-on-one or group audio/video** calls in advance.
  - Set a **date, time, and duration**.
  - **Automated reminders** via notifications, emails, or in-app alerts.
  - Supports **recurring** meetings (daily, weekly, monthly).
  - **Timezone support** shows the correct local time.
  - Option to add a **meeting agenda or notes**.
  - **Seamless joining** — participants get a direct join link at the scheduled
    time.
  - **Admin & Host Controls:** reschedule/cancel with automated updates; manage
    invitees (add/remove); set call permissions (who can join, mute others, or
    record).

## 12. ChatBucket Company — Mission, Vision, Values & Story

**ChatBucket's Mission**
To eliminate communication barriers that exclude people from meaningful
connections. Whether someone communicates through sign language, needs
visual descriptions, speaks a regional language, or has speech difficulties —
everyone deserves to participate fully in digital conversations.

**ChatBucket's Vision**
A world where disability doesn't limit connection, where language differences
spark understanding, and where diversity shapes how technology should work.

**Values**
- **Inclusion First** — If it works for the most underserved, it works for
  everyone.
- **Real Innovation** — Every line of code solves a real communication
  challenge.
- **Human Connection** — Technology should amplify human connection, not
  replace it.
- **Privacy Matters** — End-to-end encryption isn't a premium feature; it's
  a basic right.

**Our Story**
ChatBucket's founder, **Dr. Sunil Singh**, traveled widely and noticed that
most countries didn't speak English as often as people in the UK, US, and
India. Translation apps helped, but since they didn't capture emotions
properly and missed important details, conversations felt awkward.

A bigger realization followed: none of the popular communication platforms
were built for people with hearing problems, vision issues, or speech
difficulties. Poor language support combined with the exclusion of people
with sensory disabilities revealed a massive gap nobody was addressing.
That's when Dr. Singh set out to build something better — and that's how
ChatBucket was born: a platform built for **ALL**.

## 3. Feature details

### 1. Past Life Finder
When you provide your name, date of birth (DOB), birthplace, and time, the AI
shows your "past life" (where you lived, what you did, how your life was) in an
engaging story format. This is mainly a **curiosity and entertainment**-based
feature. *(In-app flow collects Full Name, Date & time of Birth, and Place of
Birth, then generates a story such as "Your Possible Past Life," with options to
"Generate another past life," "Share this story," or try the AI Palm Reader.)*

### 2. AI Palm Reader
When you upload a photo of your hand, it scans your palm lines and provides
insights about your **personality traits (confidence, leadership), career growth,
love life,** etc. It's a combination of traditional palmistry and AI. *(For
entertainment purposes; tips advise showing the entire palm clearly, keeping the
hand open and well lit, and avoiding blurry/dark photos.)*

### 3. Astrology Daily Quotes
When you provide your name, date of birth, birthplace, and time, it generates a
**complete astrology report**. After that, it gives a **daily horoscope** based
on your zodiac along with **motivational quotes** to build a positive mindset.

### 4. Smart Fare Calculator (India)
Automatically detects your current location and calculates the correct
**government-approved fare for auto, taxi, and bike rides** based on your
destination. It provides real-time fare estimates across India and helps users
**avoid overcharging**. *(Also referred to as "Auto Meter Justice" in ChatBucket
material.)*

### 5. Fast Route Suggestion
From your current location to your destination, it suggests **2–3 fastest routes**
